# In-TEA-ana Jones

As a FIRST Robotics Competition (FRC) robot, In-TEA-ana Jones is designed to compete in this year's game, "Rebuilt." In-TEA-ana Jones uses a swerve drivetrain to glide across the playing field to intake foam balls (called "fuel") and shoot them like a basketball into a bin (called the "hub"). Our Boba Bots blue bumbers proudly display our team number (253), and our polycarb panels display the logos of our sponsors who made this season possible!
