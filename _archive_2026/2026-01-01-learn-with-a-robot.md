# Bittle, Nybble Q, and an Avishkaar robot

"The Bittle is a palm-sized quadruped robot dog made by Petoi that uses nine servos and a custom Arduino-based Biboard controller. The NybbleQ is Petoi’s  robot cat made with 3D printable parts that uses eleven servos has and expressive LED eyes.  Both robots perform lifelike walking, running, and trick movements for STEM learning and hobby robotics.

The Avishkaar robot is a custom robot we designed using the sensors, brain and metallic frames from the Avishkaar Robotics Pro Kit."
